Difficulty levels1

  • What each level gives. Neutral armies and battle experience are the same on every level. Difficulty comes from starting gold, the computer's income and building, its heroes' experience and the strength of its armies in quick combat against neutrals.

    NoviceIntermediateAdvancedExpertChampion
    Your starting gold20000150001000050000
    Computer's starting gold05000100001500020000
    Computer's incomenormalnormalnormal+25%+50%
    Computer heroes' experiencenormalnormalnormal+25%+50%
    Computer skips buildingevery 3rd dayevery 6th day———
    AI vs neutrals×2×3×4×4×4
    Neutrals and battle experiencenormalnormalnormalnormalnormal

    "AI vs neutrals" is how much stronger than normal the computer's armies are in quick combat against neutrals. On Novice the computer plays a simpler AI, from Intermediate on the full one. In battle the computer fights at full strength on every level.
